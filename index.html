How to play

six guesses · one word · nothing scores until you land it

The twist

Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries. You only score points if your final guess is correct — and the longer you can hold off landing it, the more that final guess is worth.

Guess correctly on turn 1 and you bank almost nothing. Stall on real near-miss words and save the win for turn 6, and the payoff is much bigger.

Letter values

Every letter carries a Scrabble-style point value — shown in the corner of every tile, and on every key of the keyboard too, so you know what a letter is worth before you even type it.
